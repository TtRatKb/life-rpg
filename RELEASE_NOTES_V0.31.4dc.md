# Life RPG V0.31.4dc — Social Delivery & Gentle Notifications

Base: V0.31.4db. Copy the seven complete replacement/new runtime files plus these notes into the app root. Export the current Save first. Never clear site data/IndexedDB, especially while Coloring Studio artwork is stored locally.

- A new, visible **Little signs of life** dashboard card collects unread real Phone messages and newly ready Everyday Moments or genuinely unseen context-reactive Talks. The Story navigation receives a small dot for an unread Everyday Moment. All notices are spoiler-safe and open the correct existing Phone or the associated scene.
- Newly logged eligible Work/Hobbies/Recovery/Home activity can schedule **one** Living World invitation per local day. Delay is deterministic and gently varied: **18–38 minutes** after the actual completed action. The recipient must be at the canonical location and room; the scene cannot appear while they are working or otherwise unavailable. If a household moment is not eligible, an actual unseen context-reactive Talk may be offered instead. Quiet hours: **22:00–08:00** for spontaneous notifications. No alert on mere future planning, saved historical backfill, or activity predating the shared-home story gate.
- Genuine new messages from the existing Message Scheduler get a one-time in-app arrival toast. Their authored chronology, dayparts and delivery times are *not* changed by this release. Older unread messages appear in the dashboard card but are not falsely announced as just arriving.
- In-app notice is the default; device/browser notification is a separate explicit opt-in requiring permission. Device notice works only while the application is actually running (subject to browser restrictions); this release does **not** claim remote push, a server job or notifications while a fully closed PWA is suspended. Pending content catches up on reopening and remains unread until opened/completed.
- Dismissible toast plus persistent Dashboard/Phone state: no pressure to open immediately. Clearing a toast does not lose a scene or message. Existing one-per-day Everyday Moment completion, no farming of XP/Coins/Affection and immutable original scene choice all remain as before.
- Save migration adds only `socialDeliveryV1` with an initial baseline of old events/messages; does not turn historical records into a notification avalanche. Old `livingWorldV3`, messages, story state, save/cloud format, reward and timetable ledgers remain intact. Removed/retracted auto logs cancel their queued invitation.

QA performed: JavaScript syntax; initial-baseline/real-log/delay/no-duplicate/delivery/retraction/phone read/Talk fallback/quiet-hour mock tests (12 checks); asset and cache reference consistency; ZIP integrity. Real Safari/PWA and cross-device cloud Save checks require testing on the user's devices.
