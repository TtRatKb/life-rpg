Base: the **V0.31.4dn** Coloring Collection release on top of DL/DK and the user's original DJ repository. Copy the complete files in this delta into the existing repository **root**. ## Knowledge Workshop V1

- Direct access at **Spielen & Lernen → Knowledge Workshop**, as well as a Dashboard and Library entry card. The usual Life RPG sidebar and Focus Dock stay available.
- A real editable collection of **Insight Cards and Study Guides** in the additive main-save key `knowledgeWorkshopV1`, preserving original document IDs and all existing app systems. Source book selector reads the actual existing Library books; optional non-book source and page/chapter fields. Old Library notes and reading logs are neither rewritten nor invented.
- In-app title, summary and Markdown body editor with live, styled study-guide preview, toolbar for headings/emphasis/lists/quotes/tables, and tags. Preview escapes user HTML. Export an individual note or the whole collection as Markdown, including `[[linked note]]` references suitable for later Obsidian work. There is no outside AI generation or unverified academic explanation.
- Explicit labeled links between existing notes (related, supports, contrasts, example, applied). Limited but navigable visual knowledge map, accessible card listing, suggested links based on shared tags/book IDs only. Older substantial notes surface as optional Rediscover entries after a few days, without a forced streak.
- Auto-saving while writing and additive active-time measurement by second in each document/day (`timeByDay`). Normal writing detects keyboard input and pauses after 3 minutes without activity; explicitly started reading mode has a 15-minute safety stop and must be restarted. Hidden/unfocused pages and switching away do not continue running. The Activity Log displays a dated daily aggregate of actual Workshop seconds and separate actual reward transactions. No invented Work minutes.
- **No competing Focus timer:** If Precise Focus Dock is already running, the Workshop still reports active Knowledge time but does not pay time bonuses for those same seconds. The canonical work-time ledger remains authoritative. No automatic page/reading-position claims or forced reading timer for a physical book.

## Real writing-depth rewards

- Content-length milestones based on actually saved **summary + body**, with thresholds 80, 250, 500, 900, 1500, 2500 and **every additional 500 characters thereafter**. Additional reward per milestone tapers toward a positive 1 XP and 1 Coin; there is NO hard writing cutoff. XP is credited to Character + Knowledge Realm + Knowledge capability with separate ledger IDs, once per document/milestone. Character/title/tags do not inflate text length. Longer texts are worth more than short ones; no copying/plagiarism policing.
- Active Workshop time has separate modest milestones: every 5 minutes through 60, 10 minutes until 180, then 20-minute intervals, with diminishing positive rewards and no hard play cutoff. Existing Focus timer suppresses just the overlapping *time reward*, not writing rewards. Unique knowledge links receive a one-time small reward per unordered document pair; unlinking/relinking cannot re-farm it. No extra Story Energy from this module.
- **Journal:** Preserve all prior 50/150/300/600/1000-character field tiers, streak/base transactions and identities. Each extra 500 characters above 1000 in the same field continues to grant diminishing positive XP/Coins, never a hard maximum. On first load record a baseline for the existing current-day reflections so old pre-update text does not gain retroactive >1000-character rewards. New text passing a *new* milestone earns it, once; previously awarded tiers are never repeated. Previous dates remain editable without backdated reward farming. Journal UI now shows the next actual milestone instead of “Maximum writing bonus reached.”
- In Activity Log, Knowledge entries use their own filter/category and explain what was rewarded; journal explanations describe ongoing length rewards rather than an obsolete 1000-character cap.
