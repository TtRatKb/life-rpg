# Life RPG — V0.31.4ag3

## Home Talent Tree
Adds the sixth real Realm Talent Tree to the existing Skills hub.

### Nodes
- Home Compass
- Quick Reset
- Paper Trail
- Laundry Loop
- Room Rhythm
- Admin Dock
- Household Thread
- Home Base

### Real utilities
- Uses existing Home quests instead of duplicating chores.
- Reads Smart Quest signals for paper piles and laundry state.
- Starts real Life/Admin timers for Household, Appointments, Paperwork and Errands.
- Keeps short reset quests close: 10-Minute Clean, Clear One Surface, Put Away 10 Things.
- Shows recent Life Management practice and recent Life/Admin time descriptively.
- Can resume a recent matching Home route.
- Smart Home prefers actual waiting signals, then a small existing reset, otherwise simply opens Home quests.
- Home Base adds +1 Home Realm XP to the first actual Life Management practice after unlock each day.

### Guardrails
- No streak, missed-day penalty or chore quota.
- Opening a shortcut never earns Skill XP or rewards by itself.
- Existing Home quests, Habits and Focus & Time remain available without talents.
- Free respec cannot replay previously earned talent bonuses.
