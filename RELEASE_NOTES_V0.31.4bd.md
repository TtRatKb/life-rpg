# Life RPG V0.31.4bd — Social Density Pass

A content-density release for the existing story/world rather than a new main-story chapter block.

## Content
- +36 repeatable/rotating Talks across Mina, Katsuki and Eijiro.
- +15 asynchronous Message threads.
- +12 Hangouts across friendship, home, agency, gym, errands and riverside life.
- +30 World Moments spread across existing story phases and locations.
- No new Main Story chapters in this release.

## Social rotation
Repeatable Hangouts now rotate similarly to Talks. Recently played Hangouts are temporarily deprioritized so the larger pool is actually visible instead of the first repeatable Hangout monopolizing the People surface.

## Design intent
Main Story remains the milestone layer. Ordinary relationship texture should be denser than milestone scenes: little messages, errands, shared rooms, short walks, low-energy company, workday decompression, gym routines and unimportant conversations are the life around the plot.

## Compatibility
Designed as a delta over V0.31.4bc. Existing saves, story chapter progress and social history are preserved.
