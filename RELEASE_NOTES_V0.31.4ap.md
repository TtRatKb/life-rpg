# Life RPG — V0.31.4ap

## Dreamscape + real Talent Content

### Dreamscape
Each Realm now has a fourth deep branch:
**Dream Thread I → Dream Thread II**

Each Thread costs 2 Realm Points.

Thread I requires:
- Realm Special II
- the Realm's first real Content Unlock

Thread II requires:
- Dream Thread I
- Realm Special III
- deeper Content investment

Global cadence:
- 1 Thread total = dream every 14 days
- every extra Thread removes one day
- 14 / 14 Threads = dream every day
- the first Thread anywhere makes one dream available immediately
- waiting dreams never expire
- finishing a dream starts the next cooldown

The first Thread in a Realm opens that Realm's thematic dream pool.
Thread II adds more intimate variants.

Realm pools:
- Work — After Hours
- Knowledge — Quiet Minds
- Japanese — Between Words
- Health — Close Enough to Notice
- Recovery — Soft Landing
- Home — Domestic Gravity
- Hobbies — Play After Dark

Dream focus is independent:
- Bakugo
- Kirishima
- Both
- Surprise me

Dreams are explicitly non-canon:
- no Story Energy cost
- no XP / Coins
- no affinity
- no Story flags
- no changes to Luca's canon choices or Slow Burn

The Story page gets a Dreamscape card plus a replay-safe Dream Archive.
This first runtime contains 42 authored combinations: 7 Realm themes × 2 intimacy tiers × 3 focus variants.
