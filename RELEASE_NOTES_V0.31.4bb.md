# Life RPG V0.31.4bb — Familiar Distance

## Story
- Extends the installed main story from 24 to 34 chapters while preserving all existing scene IDs, choices and completed-scene history.
- Moves the Shared Apartment slow burn into a more emotionally aware phase without confessions, route locks or exposing the hidden relationship meters.
- Keeps Mina active as an independent best friend rather than reducing her to matchmaking commentary.
- Preserves the Red Riot / Dynamight fandom and rescue history as unrevealed private background; this expansion does not trigger the reveal.
- Uses the established household, Gift, Seasonal and Relationship Memory systems for contextual callbacks where the player has actually created those memories.
- Adds three later-story Story Momentum gates with flexible Work/Recovery, Home and off-duty routes. No gym or rigid productivity requirement is introduced.

## Living world
- Adds 11 new Talks, 6 new message threads, 3 new Hangouts and 5 World Moments tied to the new story phase.
- Adds new remembered preferences and support patterns from this arc to Relationship Life 2.0.
- Later callbacks remain conditional on the underlying choice/memory instead of appearing generically.

## Compatibility / QA
- `SP_003` remains the active story pack; SC_001–SC_024 are unchanged.
- A save with SC_001–SC_024 completed continues automatically at SC_025.
- Relationship-memory backfill does not modify Friendship/Affection values.
- No new canon CG is enabled or exposed; all new chapters use the installed approved VN background/sprite system.
