# Life RPG V0.31.4bc — World Expansion I

## What changed
- Extended Main Story from 34 to 44 chapters with a new neighborhood/world-expansion arc.
- Made the DynaRiot Agency a recurring everyday location rather than only an event venue.
- Introduced the private Hero Gym through story context and unlocked repeatable training/social content.
- Added Neighborhood Konbini, Neighborhood Supermarket and Riverside Park as narrative locations.
- Added new Talks, Hangouts, World Moments and Messages tied to the new locations.
- Hero Gym world moments can react to real recent Strength activity from the Life RPG reward ledger.
- Preserved Recovery as separate from Hobbies/normal activity routing; low-energy training variants can stay gentle rather than forcing hard exercise.
- Added relationship-memory hooks for training boundaries, bad-day decision fatigue, comfort-food routines and agency-visit preferences.

## Visual status
The existing approved Hero Gym background remains in use. The new Agency/Konbini/Supermarket/Park location keys are structurally integrated, but their current runtime art is an explicit temporary fallback to existing approved backgrounds. The attempted image batch produced contact-sheet/collage outputs, which fail the project's standalone-background QA rule and were **not** integrated. Native standalone renders remain a follow-up asset pass.

## Save compatibility
Existing V0.31.4bb saves continue at Chapter 35. New location keys merge into old saves without resetting prior state.
