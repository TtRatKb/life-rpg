# Life RPG V0.31.4ay1 — Gift Preference Canon Polish

A spoiler-safe data polish for Gifts & Preferences.

- Rebalanced strong gift reactions around explicit character-profile anchors and conservative character-consistent inference.
- Added new gift finds tied to established interests such as food, outdoor activity, hero fandom, hair care, dance and style.
- Removed several arbitrary strong preferences from the original AY draft; generic gifts remain useful discovery items but are no longer treated as signature favorites without support.
- Added a one-time save migration so previously discovered Gift Notes are recalculated against the polished preference model without deleting inventory, gift history or keepsakes.
- Gift economy, one-gift-per-person-per-day cap, weekly find cadence, hidden relationship gains and no-negative disliked-gift rule are unchanged.
