# Life RPG — V0.31.4an

## Talent Trees V2 — rewards you can actually feel

The original shortcut-heavy Realm trees are retired from the active build. Their old purchases are cleared once and all previously spent Realm points are refunded. Skill XP, Skill levels, Realm XP/ranks and the sources of earned Realm Points are untouched.

### Every Realm now has
- **Reward Cache I–V** — permanent one-point claims that pay Coins + Story Energy immediately and never XP.
- **Resonance I–V** — 10/20/30/40/50% chance that a real activity in that Realm procs +5 Coins +0.20 Story Energy; max 3 procs per Realm/day.
- **Momentum I–III** — first rewarded action of the day in that Realm gets +25/+50/+75% Realm XP. Character XP and Skill XP are unchanged.
- **Realm Special I–III** — a concrete effect tied to the actual Realm rather than another shortcut panel.
- An **Active Effects** strip showing exact ranks, proc counts, used daily bonuses and active charges.

### Realm specials
- Work: first completed 25m+ focus block receives a visible Coins + Story Energy bonus.
- Knowledge: first rewarded logic/knowledge puzzle receives a visible bonus.
- Japanese: first Japanese book/game immersion log receives a visible bonus.
- Health: first Journal reflection / Daily Check-in receives a visible bonus.
- Recovery: first Recovery Studio completion creates a Rested charge; the next non-Recovery action gets extra Realm XP.
- Home: first rewarded Home time block of 15m or less receives a visible bonus.
- Hobbies: first rewarded Hobbies activity receives a visible bonus.

### Real content unlocks
- Health: **Thought Untangler** — a fourth Journal reflection form. It is visibly locked before purchase and uses the existing independent Journal writing reward ladder after unlock.
- Recovery: **5–4–3–2–1 Grounding** — a new guided seven-minute Recovery Studio session, visibly locked before purchase.

Content unlocks and Reward Cache claims are permanent. Passive Resonance/Momentum/Realm-Special ranks can be respecced for free.

### Transparency
Talent Coins/Story bonuses receive their own Activity Log events and do not secretly award Character XP or Skill XP. Content nodes have a direct **Open** button after unlock. Planned content is never purchasable until the underlying feature exists.
