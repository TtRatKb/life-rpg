## Everyday actions without scheduling
- Visible Dashboard card plus small persistent `✿ Loggen` shortcut; `Alltag → Schnell loggen` offers dishwasher unloaded, laundry hung, laundry put away, trash taken out, kitchen cleared, groceries put away, fresh bed sheets, bathroom cleaned, vacuuming and a freely named one-off Home task.
- No required recurrence, daily streak, checkmark carryover or invented minutes. Real repetitions can be logged as often as done; an identical action within 90 seconds requires an explicit second confirmation to prevent accidental double-taps. Favorites (`☆/★`) move to the top.
- Direct action payout: first four non-linked Home Quick Actions on a local day each earn 2 Character XP + 2 Home Realm XP + 1 Life Management Skill XP + 1 Coin + small (0.01 base) Story Energy; subsequent actions earn 1 Character/Home XP each and still 1 Skill XP + 1 Coin. The global Story Energy daily taper may round a tiny payout to zero, without preventing XP/Coins. No hard activity cutoff. No Time/Work minutes are fabricated.
- Rewards use the canonical ledger and are auditably visible in **Activity → Home · Quick Actions**, including exact payout and explanation. Each saved record also appears in `Rückblick → Haushalt`: weekly/monthly counts, kinds and awarded XP. Existing activity filters, time ledger and other stats remain intact.
- `Wäsche aufgehängt` sets the existing Smart Quests laundry state to `drying`, and `Wäsche abgehängt & weggeräumt` sets it to `none` only when an existing Smart Quests laundry model is available. No separate laundry tracker is introduced. A recently rewarded corresponding Quest/Timer prevents a second Quick Action payout; if the quick cleanup was logged first, subsequently completing the exact 10-Minute Clean Quest retains its completion but suppresses its duplicate payout. Unrelated household work is not conflated.
- `Rückgängig` within ten minutes reverses the direct reward ledger event, removes the action from the home statistics and restores a laundry status only when the Quick Action still owns that transition. Extra random Talent/Gift add-on hooks are deliberately not run for these reversible quick actions. Old entries/saves are not recalculated or backfilled.

## Reduced navigation
- Dashboard, six collapsible areas (`Alltag`, `Zeit & Planung`, `Spielen & Lernen`, `Fortschritt`, `Story & Welt`, `Rückblick`) and a separate Settings button. Opening a group displays its hub page **and** context-specific child entries; other groups stay collapsed. The original view nodes/buttons/listeners, data-view IDs and direct page links remain intact. Training Grounds route leads to the real Growth panel; Kotoba, Coloring and Drawing are linked explicitly.
- Mobile bottom navigation is horizontally scrollable, with a separate small submenu above it. All six hubs have direct destination cards within their pages. User's existing Focus Dock and Dungeon training access remain; when a full floating timer is visible on mobile, the Quick Log shortcut moves near the top rather than hiding under it.
- No changes to Main Story, companion writing, relationship math, purchase state, Library/Games, Japanese SRS, Time ledger, previous 45-minute schedules, Story Energy economy, or cloud save transport. New additive main save key: `homeQuickActionsV1`.
